## Longer blurb (for a pinned post or the bot's "About", not the 255-char description)

🦉 Welcome to Fluent Owls
Level up your English a little every day. One set lands each evening, with a
single notification:
• ☀️ a beginner warm-up quiz (A1, A2)
• 🥪 an intermediate quiz (B1, B2)
• 🌙 an advanced challenge quiz (C1, C2)
• 📘 a grammar point, with examples you can hear
• 🗣️ a "say it like a native" phrase for a real situation
• 🎭 a role-play mini-dialogue (two voices) to act out
• 🎧 an audio clip to shadow (listen and repeat)
The quizzes and grammar make you correct; the phrase, dialogue, and shadowing
clip make you sound natural. Want more anytime? Message the bot /quiz, /grammar, /phrase,
/dialogue, /shadow, /monologue, or /prompt. No setup, no signup.
